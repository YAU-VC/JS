The Science of Gravity

This game uses Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation to simulate movement.

F = G × (M × m) / r²

How it Works

  • Mass (M): Bigger planets pull harder.
  • Distance (r²): Gravity gets much weaker as you move away. If you double the distance, gravity is 4x weaker!

The Slingshot Effect

By flying close to a planet without crashing, you can use its gravity to change direction and speed up without using fuel. Real space agencies use this "gravity assist" to send probes to Jupiter and Saturn!
