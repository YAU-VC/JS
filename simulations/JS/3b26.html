Adjust the volume and temperature to reach the target pressure (±5 kPa).

How to Play & What to Observe

  • Move the Volume slider: watch the plunger move and the chamber get smaller or larger.
  • Move the Temperature slider: gas particles bounce faster or slower.
  • Pressure increases when:
    • Volume decreases (same number of particles in a smaller space).
    • Temperature increases (particles move faster and hit the walls more often).
  • Your goal: make the Current Pressure match the Target Pressure within ±5 kPa.
  • If the pressure goes too high, the game warns that the syringe would burst!
